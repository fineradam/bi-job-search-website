Privacy Policy

This page explains how information submitted through the BI Job Search marketing website is handled.

Last updated: 4 August 2026 Operator: Vitamin Business Intelligence SASU

This policy covers the public website at bijobsearch.com. Account data and activity inside the BI Job Search application may be subject to additional information presented within the application.

Information you provide

When you use the contact form, the website may collect your name, email address, selected enquiry type and message. The form is used only to receive and respond to your enquiry.

Technical information

The hosting provider may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the website, such as IP addresses, request logs, device information and timestamps.

Why information is processed

Information may be processed to operate the website, respond to enquiries, prevent abuse, maintain security and comply with legal obligations.

Google Analytics

With your consent, this website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics may process information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser details, referral source and interactions with the website.

Analytics storage is denied by default. Google Analytics is loaded only after you select “Accept analytics.” You can withdraw or change your choice at any time using the “Cookie settings” link in the footer.

The Google Analytics measurement ID used on this website is G-VMDF171NN4. Advertising storage, ad personalisation and ad-user-data consent remain disabled.

Service providers

The website is hosted through Netlify. Information submitted through a Netlify form may be processed by Netlify to deliver the form submission to the site operator.

Retention

Contact messages are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain an appropriate record of the exchange or comply with applicable obligations.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection. Use the contact page to submit a privacy-related request.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, its service providers or its data-processing practices change.
