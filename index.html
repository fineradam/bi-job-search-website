Frequently asked questions

What you need to know before you search.

What makes BI Job Search different from LinkedIn or Indeed?

BI Job Search is built specifically for Business Intelligence professionals. It gathers jobs through targeted searches, removes duplicates and irrelevant listings, classifies genuine BI roles and extracts structured skills data from each vacancy.

Where do the jobs come from?

Jobs are gathered from multiple external sources through targeted searches. BI Job Search then processes, deduplicates, normalises and classifies the listings before displaying them.

How is my skills match score calculated?

Premium members receive a score comparing the skills selected in their profile with the skills identified in each job description. It is a skills comparison, not a prediction of hiring success.

Which countries are currently covered?

BI Job Search currently covers Business Intelligence roles in the United States, United Kingdom and Canada.

How do saved-search alerts work?

Saved searches and email alerts are Premium features. Premium members can save a filtered search and choose daily or weekly delivery for newly matching jobs.

What does Premium unlock?

Premium costs $29 for a 60-day pass and unlocks personalised skills match scores, saved searches, daily or weekly email alerts, employer names and direct application links. It is not an automatically renewing subscription.

Are the jobs exclusive to BI Job Search?

No. The underlying vacancies are publicly available elsewhere. The value of BI Job Search is in finding, cleaning, classifying, enriching and organising them specifically for Business Intelligence professionals.
