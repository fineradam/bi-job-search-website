Frequently asked questions

What you need to know before you search.

What makes BI Job Search different from LinkedIn or Indeed?

BI Job Search is built specifically for Business Intelligence professionals. It collects roles from multiple sources, applies BI-specific classification to filter out loosely related jobs and extracts structured skills data from each vacancy.

Where do the jobs come from?

Jobs are gathered from multiple external sources. BI Job Search then processes, normalises and classifies the listings before displaying genuine BI opportunities in a consistent, searchable format.

How is my skills match score calculated?

Premium members receive a score comparing the skills selected in their profile with the skills identified in each job description. It is a skills comparison, not a prediction of hiring success.

Which countries are currently covered?

BI Job Search currently covers Business Intelligence roles in the United States, United Kingdom and Canada.

How do saved-search alerts work?

Free users can save one search but do not receive email alerts. Premium users can save multiple searches and choose daily or weekly delivery for newly matching jobs.

What does Premium unlock?

Premium costs $29 for a 60-day pass and unlocks personalised skills match scores, matched and missing skills, multiple saved searches, daily or weekly alerts, employer names and direct application links. It is a one-time payment with no automatic renewal.

What happens when my 60-day Premium pass expires?

Your account automatically returns to Free. Your profile, saved jobs, view history and saved searches remain in your account, but Premium functionality becomes locked and alerts stop. You can purchase another 60-day pass whenever you need it.

Are the jobs exclusive to BI Job Search?

No. The underlying vacancies are publicly available elsewhere. The value of BI Job Search is in finding, cleaning, classifying, enriching and organising them specifically for Business Intelligence professionals.
