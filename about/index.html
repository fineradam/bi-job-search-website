About BI Job Search

A specialist platform for the Business Intelligence job market.

BI Job Search was created to make it easier for BI professionals to find genuine roles, understand employer demand and identify opportunities that fit their skills.

Why the platform exists

Business Intelligence vacancies are spread across general job sites and are often mixed with loosely related analyst, software, reporting and data roles. That leaves job seekers repeating the same searches and manually deciding which listings genuinely belong in the BI market.

BI Job Search applies a specialist classification process before displaying roles. It also extracts and categorises more than 120 skills from job descriptions, making the market easier to search and understand.

What Free users can do

Free members can search genuine BI jobs, use advanced filters, view extracted skills, explore Skills Demand, create a skills profile, save individual jobs and keep one saved search.

What Premium adds

A 60-day Premium pass adds personalised match scores, matched and missing skills, multiple saved searches, daily or weekly alerts, employer names and direct application links.

Who operates BI Job Search

BI Job Search is operated by Vitamin Business Intelligence SASU, the French company behind Learn BI Academy.
